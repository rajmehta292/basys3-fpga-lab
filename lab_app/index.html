Toolflow
One design, many representations
RTL→elaborated→netlist→placed→bitstream
Keep asking “what representation am I looking at?” Source code, synthesized logic, placed resources, and the programmed FPGA are related—but not identical.
Signal vocabulary
Words you will use every day
wire
A connection driven by combinational logic or a module output.
reg / logic
A variable used to hold a value; in clocked RTL it commonly maps to flip-flops.
posedge
A rising edge event, usually the moment registers update.
XDC
Vivado constraints: package pins, I/O standards, clocks, timing, and more.
